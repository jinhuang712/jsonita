JsonitaLoupe · precision instrument for JSON
Actual size · 860 × 560 · verdigris signal · serif reading surfaces · mono readout

Exploration, not the SoT. Jsonita as a jeweler's loupe: a precision instrument you hold up to data until the structure magnifies into clarity. Verdigris (#2f6b5d) is the signal -- oxidized copper, not tech-teal. Instrument Serif marks the reading surfaces (empty state, History and Settings titles); JetBrains Mono carries the data and the bottom readout. The inspector bar is a recessed measurement display that tracks the selected node like a caliper.
